Privacy Policy

Recs. Last updated: November 17, 2026. Effective: November 17, 2026.

The short version

  • Recs is a private place to swap movie, TV, book, music and podcast recommendations with people you invite.
  • We collect only what the app needs to work: your account email, the name you choose, and the recs, reactions and replies you create.
  • We never read your address book, never sell your data, never show ads and never track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account and your data from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

Benjamin Fishman, Walnut Creek, California, USA ("we", "us"). Contact: bfishman1992@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account info. Your email address (or an Apple private relay address if you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email) and the display name you enter or that Apple shares the first time you sign in. Optional profile photo.
  • Content you create. Recs you send or save, notes, reactions, star ratings, replies, reports you file, people you block, and invite links you create.
  • Friend connections. Who you are connected with after an invite is accepted.
  • Push notification token. If you turn on notifications, a device token from Apple so we can deliver them, plus your notification settings.
  • Searches. When live search is on, the title you type is sent to Apple's iTunes Search API to look up matches. Apple's privacy policy applies to that request. We do not store your searches on our servers.
  • Cover art. To show covers, our server (or, in sample friends mode, your phone) sends a title's name, creator and type to Apple's iTunes Search API, Open Library or Podcast Index. Nothing that identifies you is sent.

We do not collect your contacts, location, advertising identifier, browsing history, or health or financial data.

What stays on your phone

Voice notes you record and recs you saved from people who are not on Recs ("heard from") are stored only on your device unless a future version asks you to share them. Speech you dictate is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition on your device where supported; Apple may process it on its servers. We do not receive the audio.

How we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, deliver recs to the people you choose, show reactions and replies, and send notifications you turned on.
  • To keep the community safe: review reports within 24 hours, remove content that breaks our Terms, and suspend accounts that abuse others.
  • To fix problems: limited server logs kept for up to 30 days.

We do not use your data for advertising or to train AI models, and we do not sell or rent it.

Who can see your content

Only the people you send a rec to can see it, along with your reactions and replies on recs they sent you. People you block cannot see your activity or send you recs, and they are not told. Our service providers process data on our behalf: Supabase (database, authentication, file storage; United States) and Apple (Sign in with Apple, push notifications). We may disclose information if required by law.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. Delete your account in Settings, Account, Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, the recs you received, your reactions, invites, blocks and device tokens within minutes, and revokes the Sign in with Apple connection. Recs you already sent and replies you wrote stay with the friends who received them, shown as from "Deleted user", with your personal notes removed. Backups roll off within 30 days. You can also download a copy of your data from the same screen first.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export and delete your data in the app, or by emailing bfishman1992@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have additional rights, including to object to processing or to complain to a data protection authority. California residents may request access to or deletion of their personal information, and we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law.

Children

Recs is not directed to children under 13 (or 16 where local law requires), and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access to each record is limited by row level security so that only you and the friends you share with can read it.

Changes

We will post updates here and tell you in the app before material changes take effect.
